Your privacy & control

The AI can help with your work, but it does not control your account.

You can ask the assistant to help with tasks, daily todos, habits, challenges and timers. Before the app makes a change, it checks that the action is allowed and belongs to your account. Sensitive settings such as your password, email address and account deletion stay in your hands.

About the creator

Thembinkosi Eden Thwala

M.O.B TaskManager was designed and developed by Thembinkosi Eden Thwala as a full-stack portfolio project focused on bringing planning, focused work and personal growth into one practical digital workspace.

The application combines task and Todo management, persistent time tracking, Pomodoro sessions, habit building, reading challenges, project tracking, account security and an action-capable AI assistant. It is built to work across the web, desktop and mobile while keeping sensitive credentials on the server.

The project reflects a commitment to thoughtful product design, secure backend engineering and technology that feels useful in everyday life.
